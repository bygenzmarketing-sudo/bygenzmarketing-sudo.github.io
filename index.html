FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to see results?

For social media, you can start seeing engagement within 2-4 weeks. For SEO, it typically takes 2-3 months for noticeable ranking improvements. Paid ads can generate leads within the first week of launch.

What's the minimum budget to get started?

There's no strict minimum. I work with businesses of all sizes and customize plans based on your budget. Even ₹3,000–5,000/month can get you started with basic social media or SEO.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes! I work with clients globally. Intel Safari Travel is a US-based client. All communication happens over email and video calls, so location is never a barrier.

Can I hire you for just one service (like only SEO)?

Absolutely! You can pick any single service — SEO only, social media only, just paid ads, or just a website. No need to commit to a full package.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Just your business details, goals, and any existing social media/website access. I handle everything else — from strategy to execution. We start with a free discovery call.
